Ascension Day always falls on a Thursday, 39 days after Easter Sunday. In 2027 that is **Thursday 6 May**, and it's a public holiday in both Sweden (Kristi himmelfärdsdag) and every German state (Christi Himmelfahrt). The Friday after it, 7 May, is the classic bridge day: one leave day turns it into a four-day weekend from Thursday to Sunday. This guide covers the dates, the longer versions, and trips that suit four days in early May.

## Why Ascension matters more than usual in 2027

In Sweden, 2027 is a poor year for spring holidays. May Day (1 May) is a Saturday and National Day (6 June) is a Sunday, and Sweden doesn't move holidays that fall on a weekend. Easter gives four days but no bridge. That makes the Friday after Ascension the only bridge day in the first half of the year, and the best-value leave day Swedes have until Midsummer.

Germany does better: May Day is a Saturday there too, but Whit Monday follows on 17 May, and six states have Corpus Christi on Thursday 27 May, which is a second Thursday-plus-bridge weekend. We cover those in our [Pentecost and Corpus Christi guide](/guides/pentecost-corpus-christi-2027-germany/).

## The dates

| Sweden: Thu 6 – Sun 16 May | 11 | 7 May and 10–14 May (6) |
| Germany: Thu 6 – Mon 17 May | 12 | 7 May and 10–14 May (6) |

The difference between the two long versions is Whit Monday. It's a public holiday in Germany but hasn't been one in Sweden since 2005, when National Day replaced it, so the Swedish version ends on Whit Sunday, 16 May.

Many employers in both countries close on the Friday after Ascension anyway, and many schools give it as a day off too, so check before you spend a leave day on it. ## What four days in early May is good for

Early May is a sweet spot for short trips: the days are long, Southern Europe is warm without being hot, and the summer crowds haven't arrived. Typical daytime highs in early May are around 20–23 °C in Rome, Barcelona, Lisbon and Mallorca, and around 24–27 °C in Athens and Seville. Northern Europe is cooler and less predictable, often 13–18 °C.

The rule we use for a four-day break: **no more than about three hours of travel each way**, and ideally leave on Wednesday evening or Thursday morning. With one long travel day in each direction you've turned four days into two.

### From Sweden

- **Copenhagen** is the easiest trip from southern Sweden, by train over the bridge, and a good one in May, when the outdoor seating along the harbour and in the parks fills up.
- **Berlin, Amsterdam and Prague** are all under two hours by plane from Stockholm or Gothenburg. Amsterdam in early May has the end of the tulip season; Keukenhof usually runs into mid-May.
- **Mallorca, Nice and Rome** are around three to three and a half hours from Stockholm. Mallorca in early May is warm, green and much calmer than in summer; it's a good time for walking in the Serra de Tramuntana or cycling.
- **Edinburgh** works if you prefer cool weather and hills. Note that travellers from the EU now need an ETA (electronic travel authorisation) to visit the UK, so apply in advance.
- **Gotland** is the domestic option: the ferry from Nynäshamn to Visby takes around three hours, and in early May the island is quiet, with the medieval town walls of Visby largely to yourself. Many restaurants keep short hours before the summer season, so check before you go.

### From Germany

- **By train:** Ascension is a holiday in Austria, most of Switzerland, France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Denmark and Norway too, so neighbouring countries have the same long weekend and the trains are full. Book early. Good four-day trips include Vienna or Salzburg from Munich, Paris from Frankfurt or Cologne, Amsterdam or Brussels from the Rhine and Ruhr, and Copenhagen from Hamburg.
- **By plane:** Lisbon, Porto, Mallorca, Nice, Rome and Split are all around two to three hours from the big German airports. Croatia's coast in early May is quiet and pleasant, though the sea is still cool.
- **Close to home:** the Moselle, the Rhine gorge and the Saxon Switzerland national park are at their best in spring and easy to reach by regional train.

Note the German tradition: Ascension Day is also Father's Day (Vatertag or Herrentag), when groups of men set off on walks with a handcart of drinks. Rural paths and regional trains can be busy and loud on the Thursday. If you're looking for quiet walking, the Friday is calmer.

## The long version: nearly two weeks for six leave days

If you can spare six days, the stretch from Ascension to Whitsun is one of the best of the year. In Germany it's 12 days, from 6 to 17 May; in Sweden it's 11, from 6 to 16 May. That's enough for something that doesn't fit in a long weekend:

- **A Greek island hop.** Ferries between the islands start running more often from May. Crete and Rhodes are warm by early May; the Cyclades are pleasant but windier.
- **Portugal end to end.** Fly into Porto, take the train south to Lisbon, and finish on the Algarve, where the beaches are quiet before June.
- **Andalusia.** Seville, Córdoba and Granada are at their best in May, before the summer heat. Granada's Alhambra needs a timed ticket, and those sell out weeks ahead.
- **Sicily or Sardinia.** Both are green and warm in May, with prices well below summer.

## Practical tips for the Ascension weekend

1. **Book the outbound early, not the inbound.** Wednesday evening and Thursday morning are the busiest departures of the weekend in both countries. Sunday evening is the busiest return. A Sunday morning or midday return is often cheaper and less stressful.
2. **Check what's open where you're going.** Ascension is a holiday in many countries but not all. In Spain, Italy, Portugal, Greece and Croatia it's a normal working day, which is an advantage: shops, museums and restaurants keep normal hours.
3. **Pack for two seasons.** Early May can swing from warm to cold in a day, even in the south. A light warm layer and a rain jacket cover most cases.
4. **Don't waste a leave day you'd get anyway.** If your employer closes on the bridge day, the planner can mark it as a day off, and your leave goes to a different break instead.

The short version: book Friday 7 May now, decide between a city and the sun, and keep the trip within three hours of home.
